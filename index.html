Dr. Vamanie Perumal

Decision Sciences & Artificial Intelligence Researcher

I develop interpretable computational approaches to prediction, counterfactual analysis, performance assessment and resource allocation, spanning explainable AI, visual analytics, optimization and causal inference. My applications include online advertising, transportation, emergency medical services, energy systems and entrepreneurial ecosystems.

My PhD at IIT Madras (Engineering Design) investigated online advertising effectiveness through geo-experimental design. My work combines funded research, academic–industry collaboration, mentoring and teaching.

Application areas

Online advertising

Geo-experiments, causal attribution, lift prediction and counterfactual estimation on billion-scale retail and advertising time series.

Transportation & public services

Multistage performance benchmarking of Indian Railways (800+ routes), Tamil Nadu bus transport and emergency medical services (3.59M call records).

Energy & buildings

Forecasting, anomaly detection and XAI-driven chiller-plant optimization on multisite HVAC data for building decarbonization.

Water & health

Machine-learning prediction of uranium in Punjab groundwater; Raman spectroscopy + ML for authenticating herbal medicines.

Industrial AI

Vision-language models and few-shot learning for bearing-defect detection; LLMs for automated design and testing of control panels.

Entrepreneurial ecosystems

Decade-long analysis and visualization of Indian startup performance with CREST, YNOS and MeitY.
